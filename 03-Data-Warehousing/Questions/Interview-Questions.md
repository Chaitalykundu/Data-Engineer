## Data warehousing and Data Modeling

### 1. how does data warehousing relate to data modeling

Data Warehousing is the process of storing integrated historical data for analytics, while Data Modeling is the technique used to organize that data efficiently within the warehouse. Data modeling defines structures such as fact tables, dimension tables, star schemas, and snowflake schemas, enabling faster queries, better reporting, and easier analysis.
