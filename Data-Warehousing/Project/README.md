# Choose a domain (Retail, HR, Sales) for your mini DW project

Retail Data Warehouse

&nbsp;

## Why?

- Most common interview domain.
- Covers customers, products, sales, inventory, and stores.
- Easy to understand and explain.
- Demonstrates dimensional modeling effectively.

&nbsp;

## Business Questions

- Total sales by month?
- Top-selling products?
- Revenue by region?
- Customer lifetime value?
- Return percentage?

&nbsp;

## Skills Covered

- ETL
- Star Schema
- Slowly Changing Dimensions (SCD)
- Aggregations
- BI Reporting
