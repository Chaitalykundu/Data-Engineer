1. What is the difference between a database, schema and warehouse?

   Key points: logical data organization, object containers, independent compute resources.

2. Why configure auto-suspend and auto-resume?

   Key points: reduce idle compute consumption and make the warehouse available on demand.

3. How would you investigate a missing table?

   Key points: object name, database/schema context, metadata visibility, privileges and role hierarchy.

4. Why did your reporting table not change after an employee's salary was updated in the raw table?

   Key points: CTAS creates a snapshot; it is not a continuously refreshed pipeline.

5. How do you verify data quality after loading?

   Key points: row counts, nulls, duplicates, unmatched keys, expected totals and source-to-target reconciliation.
